OpenSeeFace bundled with OpenVT Delta for Windows
================================================

This distribution includes OpenSeeFace 1.20.5 by Emiliana, provided as a
separate, unmodified executable. OpenVT Delta for Windows starts it to process webcam frames
locally and receive facial tracking parameters over loopback UDP.

OpenSeeFace is licensed under the BSD 2-Clause License. Its copyright,
license conditions and disclaimer are preserved in openseeface/LICENSE
and openseeface/Licenses/OpenSeeFace.txt. The upstream notices in
openseeface/Licenses and additional runtime notices in
openseeface/AdditionalLicenses are part of this distribution.

Upstream release:
https://github.com/emilianavt/OpenSeeFace/releases/tag/v1.20.5

DShowCapture
------------

The separate dshowcapture_x64.dll and dshowcapture_x86.dll libraries are
licensed under GNU LGPL version 2.1. The license is preserved in
openseeface/Licenses/DShowCapture.txt. Both unmodified DLLs exactly match
libdshowcapture release v20250803, source revision:
b6b35fc5d8fe17c8b1c7029f37a05622d65ee810

The complete corresponding library source, including its license and
build project files, is supplied in:
openseeface/CorrespondingSource/libdshowcapture-b6b35fc5-source.zip

Source repository:
https://github.com/emilianavt/libdshowcapture/tree/v20250803

Users may replace them with interface-compatible modified builds.
